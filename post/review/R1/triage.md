The five finding files are `N-narrative.md`, `L-lean.md`, `C-citations.md`,
`G-figures.md` and `Y-style.md`. The decisions below apply to every finding in a file
unless the finding is listed by name.

**Accept.** The edit is made in the apply step (A).
- The order of passes is N → G → L, C → Y.
- Each pass is limited to the changes `post/REVIEW.md` allows it.
- Any edit that changes what a sentence claims goes through the diff check (D).

**Reject.** A reason is given.

**Defer.** The finding is recorded in `post/TODO.md`.

## Rules for every pass

1. **A mark sits on exactly what its theorem says.**
   - If a sentence says more, either split it (marked part, then unmarked part) or
     narrow it.
   - Hypotheses the theorem needs are stated in words where a reader needs them (for
     example: finishing runs, a well-formed table, a normal form reached, closed trees).
2. **Numbers and pictures computed by the essay's own reducer are labelled as such.**
   These include interaction counts, the three interactions until `a` is read, ten
   interactions, and "no repeat in 1000 interactions". They are not marked as theorems.
   The Mechanized section gets a short paragraph listing what the browser computes.
3. **tree-semantics results are scoped to what they state.** That means closed trees,
   `Tree.Eval`, strict evaluation, and the specific lazy example with a leaf output. The
   open-term, free-wire behaviour in the figures is the browser model.
4. **Claims that are argued but not mechanized stay in the text,** worded at their
   strength, and are listed under "Not mechanized". This covers:
   - the tree route identifications;
   - tree absorption of the lap;
   - net holes and repetition;
   - a global net counter;
   - the full code-reading characterization in tree calculus and nets;
   - "runs to I" by a probe in the open tree calculus;
   - the factorization system generated by the steps.
5. **Nothing is weakened silently.** Where a figure or note said something false, the
   correction says what is true. Examples: the net fibre graph has commuting squares,
   so there are round trips; the net counter claim; the loop following the curated
   cycle.
