# 9. Where higher coherence calms down

The hope, in a friend's words: "like manifolds, eventually once you get high
enough there's something that makes everything calm down." Is there a bounded
arena of notions of computation that the knobs move within?

The answer has two parts.

## 9.1 Invertible coherence does calm down

The arena is `Pr^L`: presentable ∞-categories with left adjoints.

- [MEMORY] Every presentable ∞-category is a localization of presheaves, and
  localization at a set of maps always exists there (Lurie). So the "does the
  reflector exist" question has a clean answer, and local-ness is a property
  with no strict/pseudo/lax split.
- [CITED] **Modes.** Idempotent commutative algebras in `Pr^L` are called
  modes. They capture aspects that are both property and structure: pointed,
  semiadditive, stable.
- [CITED] **Truncation is a mode.** n-truncated spaces form a presentable
  idempotent, and its modules are exactly the locally n-truncated presentable
  ∞-categories.
- [MEMORY] **Enrichment.** Enrichment in `V` is `V`-modules in `Pr^L`.

This reframes the original complaint that truncation causes the trouble:
truncation is a localization of the arena itself, so the untruncated arena is
the calm object, and each truncation level is a mode of it.

## 9.2 Direction does not close at a finite height, but the tower is regular

- [CITED] Stefanich: presentable (∞,n)-categories assemble into a presentable
  (∞,n+1)-category, not an (∞,n)-category.
- [CITED] Each level is the endomorphisms of the unit of the next. Such a
  sequence is a **categorical spectrum**.

So the calm is the kind you get from a spectrum, not a manifold: the whole
tower is a single stable object.

Compare the two outcomes for associativity (`04` §4.2): impose the pentagon
and stop at the Cat level, or leave it free and get the completely regular
A∞ tower. And for adjunctions (`07` §7.1): the higher tower is contractible.

## 9.3 A conjecture-shaped statement [SPEC]

A model of formal computation is a module over an algebra `V` in that tower,
equipped with a factorization system generated by small steps.

Guess: two directed levels suffice, time and space (the double-categorical
structure of `02` §2.2 and `05`). Lax factorization systems at that level are
the missing piece.

## 9.4 Controls for the cost of higher structure

Each enrichment level adds a dimension to the tower of `02` §2.5. Two controls:

- **Truncation.** The maps spaces → categories → posets → sets are
  change-of-base maps in the tower. Work at the lowest level that sees your
  overlaps.
- **Formal category theory.** Equipments or ∞-cosmoi handle coherence once,
  instead of level by level.

The first post stays at the Set and Cat levels.
