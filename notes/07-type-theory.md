# 7. Type theory in the framework

Intended for a second post. Mostly [HAND] and [SPEC]; references mostly
[MEMORY].

## 7.1 Rules as adjunctions

**Refined claim.** A rule is property-like when it is the unit or counit of a
**local adjunction** (an adjunction between hom-categories) to a structural
map. [MEMORY] The notion is Jay's; Seely, and Jay and Ghani, use it for
λ-calculus.

- **Functions.** "Apply to a fresh variable" `C(Γ, A ⇒ B) → C(Γ × A, B)` has
  λ as right adjoint. Counit: β. Unit: η-expansion.
- **Products.** Projecting `C(Γ, A × B) → C(Γ, A) × C(Γ, B)` has pairing as
  right adjoint. Same pattern.
- **Unit.** `⟨⟩` is terminal in `C(Γ, 1)`.

Consequences.

- **No collapse.** There can be many 2-cells from redex to contractum, but β
  is canonically the universal one. The structure is property-like in Kelly
  and Lack's sense, because adjoints are unique.
- **A prediction confirmed.** If β is a reduction, adjointness forces η to be
  an expansion. Rewriting theorists found this empirically (Jay and Ghani,
  "The virtues of eta-expansion").
- **Triangle laws** are exactly what resolves the β/η critical pairs. They
  declare the loop `f a ⇒ (λx. f x) a ⇒ f a` to be the identity, killing a
  would-be divergence one dimension up.
- **Why SKI differs.** K and S reify weakening and contraction as constants,
  so there is no structural map left to be adjoint to.
- **Calm-down for adjunctions.** [MEMORY] Riehl and Verity: an adjunction
  extends to a homotopy coherent one in an essentially unique way. So for
  presentations whose rules are adjunctions, the higher tower is contractible.

## 7.2 Proposed definition

> A presentation is **type-theoretic** when
> 1. its rules are units and counits of local adjunctions to structural maps;
> 2. the family of adjunctions is stable under substitution;
> 3. (for dependent types) computations pull back along type families
>    (Frobenius) and `L` is left exact (`08`).

[SPEC] as a definition. Checked by hand on simply typed λ-calculus
(`04` §4.4).

## 7.3 Polarity

- **Negative types** are right adjoints to structural maps: functions,
  records, unit. They are defined by how they are probed. Both β and η are
  stable under substitution, so both can be definitional.
- **Positive types** are left adjoints: sums, naturals, inductive types. They
  are defined by how they are built. Their stability in context fails
  strictly (it needs distributivity/Frobenius), so only β is definitional.
  For sums, commuting conversions are the price of η.

This matches practice (η for records but not for data). It is the
built-versus-probed polarity of `02` §2.2, and tree calculus sits entirely on
the positive side (`04` §4.6).

## 7.4 The classifier

In Awodey's natural models, types and terms are presheaves on contexts, and
each type former is defined once on the universal family `Tm → Ty`. Stability
under substitution is then naturality.

So the classifier is what makes adjunction rules stable. This connects the
self-classification knob (`02` §2.4) to coherence:

- an external classifier (a presheaf over contexts) is the weakest setting;
- a universe makes it partly representable, so the classifier becomes a probe;
- type-in-type makes it fully so, and Girard's paradox produces a loop that
  nothing absorbs.

**Arities.** Normal forms are stable under renaming but not under
substitution. So normalization is representability of the value against
renaming-probes only, which is why gluing proofs use them.

**Adding rules.** [MEMORY] Cockx, Tabareau and Winterhalter's type theory with
user rewrite rules needs a confluence check to keep typing sound. That is the
coherence condition on `S`.

## 7.5 Dependent type theory has two fibres

**The conversion rule.** If `a` reduces to `a′` then `B(a)` and `B(a′)` are
the same type, with no witness. In the framework: a dependent type is a
behaviour over the reduction space of its context, with trivial monodromy
imposed by fiat.

**So definitional equality is a 0-truncation**: the presentation pushed down
to `V = Set`, where a type cannot see how a term was reduced.

**Identity types are the other fibre.** Transport along a path is real data,
and monodromy is allowed. [MEMORY] Gambino and Garner: identity types give a
weak factorization system, where the computational one is orthogonal.

**The seam.** Known pain points sit where the two fibres meet: transport
bookkeeping, strictness problems in models, the need for two-level type
theories.

**The second fibre cannot be removed.** `n + 0 = n` is provable by induction,
but for a variable `n` the two sides are not connected by any zigzag of
computations (`04` §4.5). Identifications are not generated by computations.
The extra ones come from the η side of positive types.

**Consequence for §7.2.** Identity types and inductive types satisfy their
η-laws only propositionally, so Martin-Löf type theory is not type-theoretic
in the strict sense. The definition has to be relative: rules are adjunctions
up to a chosen weaker fibre.

**Double-categorical reading** [SPEC]. Definitional equality behaves like the
tight direction and identification like the loose direction. Every
definitional equality has a companion path (reflexivity). The converse is
equality reflection, which collapses the two and costs decidability.

**Sheaf reading** [CONJ]. Identifications are computations sheafified along
constructor covers (`04` §4.5).

## 7.6 A design: proof-relevant, directed conversion

**Judgments.**

- The usual three: `Γ ctx`, `Γ ⊢ A type`, `Γ ⊢ a : A`.
- No conversion judgment for computation rules. Definitional equality
  survives only for substitution bookkeeping.
- One new type former: for `a, a′ : A` a type of reductions `a ⇒ a′`. A
  reduction is a term `ρ : a ⇒ a′`.
- Coherence between parallel reductions is an identification in the reduction
  type. No new judgment; the tower closes by reusing identity types.

**Rules for reductions.**

- Identity and composition, with category laws as identifications.
- Congruence: every term former acts on reductions; interchange and
  naturality hold as identifications (permutation equivalence).
- Generators: β for every type former; η-expansion for negative ones.
- Triangle laws for each β/η pair, as identifications.
- Companion: every `ρ : a ⇒ a′` gives a path `⌜ρ⌝ : a = a′`, functorially.
  There is no map back.
- Coercion along `ρ` is transport along `⌜ρ⌝`. So type families are
  automatically local, and the contravariance of function types in their
  domain is fine because paths invert.
- Positive types: β is a reduction; induction yields identifications only.
- **Required addition** (found by the ℕ test): coercion must compute by
  recursion on the type former, or closed terms get stuck (`08`).

**Version 1.** Declare reduction types to be sets. That is the Cat level, and
every test so far passes there.

**What would have to be proved.**

1. Coherence: on the negative fragment, reduction types from a term to its
   normal form are contractible. Then the system is conservative over the
   usual one there, with coercions inserted by elaboration. [MEMORY] Curien,
   Garner and Hofmann did the analogous thing for substitution.
2. Canonicity with trace: a closed natural number reduces to a numeral by a
   reduction unique up to identification.
3. Erasure: collapsing reductions to equalities is change of base to Set and
   returns ordinary type theory.

**Tests so far.**

- Unit and functions: passes at level 1 (`04` §4.4).
- Naturals: fibres separate as predicted; canonicity needs the coercion rules
  (`04` §4.5).

**Closest prior art** [MEMORY].

- Objective type theory (van den Berg, den Besten): removes definitional
  computation and makes every rule a path. Here they are directed instead.
- calf (Niu, Sterling, Grodin, Harper): a dependent type theory with a cost
  effect and a modality that erases cost. That modality is the localization
  `L`.
- Riehl and Shulman: directed hom-types alongside identity types.
- Licata and Harper's 2-dimensional directed type theory; Ahrens, North and
  van der Weide's comprehension bicategories: reductions as 2-cells.
- Cubical type theory moves more of the path fibre into computation.

What seems new: a hom-type whose generators are the theory's own rules,
presented as units and counits.

**Risks.**

- Transport everywhere, if coherence fails.
- The reduction type is an indexed higher inductive family over syntax; its
  good behaviour is unchecked.
- If coherences should themselves be directed, the lax theory is needed.

## 7.7 The untruncated ideal [SPEC]

A presentation over spaces whose rules are local adjunctions with a
classifier, where types are local systems on the directed reduction space. No
existing system is known to us to do all of this.
