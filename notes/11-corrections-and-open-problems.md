## 11.3 Open problems

1. **The lax theory.** Factorization systems one directed level up, so that
   coherences between reductions can themselves be directed. Connect lax
   orthogonal factorization systems to rewriting inside the arities setting.
2. **Higher levels of the reduction type.** All coherence results used are
   about 2-cells as sets. Does the reduction type, as a higher inductive
   family, have higher homotopy on the negative fragment?
3. **The sheafification conjecture.** Identifications are computations
   sheafified along constructor covers. Make "constructor cover" a
   Grothendieck topology on contexts and prove or refute.
4. **Proof-relevant dependent type theory** (`07` §7.6). Coherence,
   canonicity with trace, erasure.
5. **Ω in full SKI** without assuming residual theory.
6. **Classification.** For which knob settings does a fibre of the tower have
   a single top class under simulation?
7. **The three theses as sub-double-categories** (`05` §5.7). Make precise.
8. **Presentations as models of a double theory** (`05` §5.8).
9. **Frobenius and exactness** as the definition of a type-theoretic
   presentation: which of the examples satisfy both?
10. **Abstract interpretation** as the adjunction of Theorem B (`05` §5.4).
11. **A finer directed invariant** than `End(t) → π₁`, perhaps from directed
    topology.
12. **Realizability** enters through `Φ`, not `S`. Are these two knobs or one?
