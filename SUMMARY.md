# Summary: computation as a factorization system

Status labels are explained in `README.md`. [LEAN] means proved in Lean in this repo
(declarations in `PROOF_MAP.md`, axioms in `AXIOMS.md`); everything else is as labelled.

## The thesis

Computation is a factorization system on descriptions. Denotation is its
localization. Holes show up as monodromy.

A thing given by a presentation has many descriptions. The descriptions form a
space fibred over the things they describe. Taking connected components
recovers the denotation. Computation is the directed geometry of the fibres,
which taking components forgets. The input-output function that computer
science usually treats as primary is the shadow; the fibre is the object.

## The definition

A **presentation** is a category `P` of descriptions with a class `S` of maps,
the **steps**. In the main example `P` is the category of presheaves on a
category `A` of terms and reductions: a term `t` is described by `y(t)`, and
`S` is the generating rewrites `y(t) → y(t′)`. Computations run forward;
an observation acts on a step backwards, by Yoneda. Covariant families
`A ⥤ Type`, the setting for type families and coercion, give a second
presentation with the same theorems.

From `(P, S)` everything else is derived.

- `M` is the class of maps with unique right lifting against `S`. `E` is the
  class with unique left lifting against `M`.
- **Computations** are the maps in `E`.
- **Behaviours** are the objects `X` with `X → 1` in `M`. Equivalently, the
  `S`-local objects.
- **Evaluation** of `X` is the `(E, M)`-factorization `X → LX → 1`. The first
  map is the big step.
- A **trace** is a decomposition of `X → LX` into pushouts of steps. That is
  small-step evaluation.
- **Conversions** are the maps inverted by `L`. When the presentation is
  convergent (every map factors), they are the 2-out-of-3 closure of `E`.

The model is the pair `(P, E)`. Keeping only the behaviours loses the
direction.

## What the definition explains

| Rewriting notion | In the framework |
|---|---|
| Normal form | Terminal object of a fibre; a local object |
| Normalization | The unit `X → LX` |
| Confluence | Ore condition; calculus of fractions |
| Knuth–Bendix completion | Adding steps already in `E`, which changes nothing |
| Coherence theorem | Reduction space has contractible components |
| Denotational semantics | Change of base to sets |
| Complexity | Minimal cost of a trace |
| Coercion along a reduction | The unique diagonal filler |

## Main results

1. **Theorem A** [LEAN]. In the dynamic picture, behaviours are the
   presheaves on which every step acts bijectively, equivalently the functors
   out of the localization at the steps: local systems. Proved for presheaves
   and for covariant families. That the localization is a groupoid (when the
   steps generate `A`) is [HAND].
2. **Theorem B** [LEAN]. A simulation, taken as an adjunction `G ⊣ R` whose
   left adjoint sends steps to computations, induces an adjunction between
   behaviour categories (`L′ ∘ G ⊣ R`). Uniting rule sets intersects
   behaviours. Adding rules that are already conversions changes no
   behaviour; adding rules already in `E` changes neither `E` nor `C`.
   Translations via left Kan extension, and conjoints, are [HAND].
3. **Collapse lemma** [LEAN]. Lax orthogonality to a single free rule forces a
   unique arrow from each redex to its contractum; in fact it is equivalent
   to that, for a left adjoint section with identity unit, and the collapse
   persists for invertible units and right adjoint sections. So rule cells
   are structure, and direction has to live in the factorization system.
   That this is Clementino and López Franco's notion is a citation check.
4. **Associativity** [LEAN for the loop, HAND for its shape]. With one
   oriented associativity rule and no pentagon, the two routes from
   `((xy)z)w` to `x(y(zw))` stay distinct after inverting all reductions,
   and a behaviour detects the difference through a ℤ-valued weight [LEAN].
   That the reduction space is a circle and evaluation a ℤ-torsor is [HAND].
   Over the Tamari order, which has a top element, behaviours are bare sets
   [LEAN]; that imposing the pentagon gives the Tamari order is [MEMORY]
   (Laplaza).
5. **SKI** [LEAN]. The count of S-steps on the right spine is invariant under
   permutation equivalence on K-free terms, so it is a 1-cocycle. It gives the
   loop `SII(SII) → SII(SII)` the value 1, so no continuation absorbs it:
   that divergence is unavoidable. The loop inside `K a Ω` is absorbed by the
   K-step, so that divergence is avoidable. Permutation equivalence is the one
   generated by the elementary squares in Lean; that it is Lévy's is [MEMORY].
6. **Groups** [LEAN]. For a group presentation, `n` relator deletions take `w`
   to `1` exactly when `w` is a product of `n` conjugates of relators, so
   minimal trace cost equals area and the Dehn function is the complexity
   function.
7. **Coercion** [LEAN for the lifting facts, HAND for the rest]. Coercion along
   a computation is the unique filler against a type family; it inverts
   restriction and is functorial. Its rules for substitution, pairs and
   equality are closure properties of `M` (pullback, composition, diagonal).
   Functions need a Frobenius condition, and the universe needs `L` to be
   left exact [HAND].

## Findings worth a second post

- **A trichotomy for loops** [HAND]. The map `End(t) → π₁` separates coherence
  defects, avoidable divergence and unavoidable divergence.
- **Approximation is derived** [HAND]. With a calculus of fractions, the value
  of a term is the ind-object of its reducts. A normal form exists when that
  ind-object is representable.
- **Type theories** [SPEC, partly HAND]. A type theory is a presentation whose
  rules are units and counits of local adjunctions, stable under substitution.
  Polarity predicts which η-laws can be definitional.
- **Dependent type theory has two fibres** [HAND]. Definitional equality is a
  0-truncated orthogonal system. Identity types are a weak one. Known pain
  points sit on the seam.
- **Double category of presentations** [HAND]. Translations are tight and
  simulations loose. A translation has a conjoint exactly when it is
  bisimulation-like.
- **The arena** [CITED]. Invertible coherence calms down in presentable
  ∞-categories, where truncation levels are modes. Direction does not close at
  a finite height, but the tower is regular.
